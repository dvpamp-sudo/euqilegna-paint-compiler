Beta Test Plan

Tester Instructions

  1. Choose one design or upload one image.
  2. Paint for at least 15 minutes.
  3. Save, close the browser, reopen, and verify progress returns.
  4. Use zoom, pan, hints, skip color, and Show Next Section.
  5. Finish a small design when possible and review the completed artwork.
  6. Submit the feedback form—even when the experience went well.

Suggested Tester Groups

Invite 5 family members, 5 friends, 5 creative hobbyists, and 5 people who have never used paint-by-number software.

Run on your local network

Start the server with:

C:\PythonEnvs\euqilegna\Scripts\python.exe -m uvicorn main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000

People connected to the same Wi-Fi can use your computer’s local IPv4 address followed by :8000/beta. Windows Firewall may ask for permission.
